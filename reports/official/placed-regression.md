## Caveats

- n = 5, and the fit spends 2 degrees of freedom.
- Every component correlates with placed above r = 0.96, so the residuals are small differences of large numbers.
- Correlation is not mechanism. A component could genuinely measure what its name says AND track placed.
- The observed num_placed_items range across the five submissions is 0.434 to 0.505. Evaluating a fit outside it is arithmetic, not prediction.
