## Why preference distillation succeeded where cloning failed

Same corpus, same architecture, same arena — only the objective
changed. Behavior cloning (match 002) reproduced the incumbent 90 % of
the time and executed as pure rank-0 (0 wins). The preference head's
group-OOF profile (pair AUC 0.745; at τ=0.5: 92.6 % of quiet roots
untouched, 7.4 % false-switch, 24 % intervention-action recall) is
deliberately asymmetric — it deviates rarely, and the deviations it
kept were real: one held-out Pareto win, zero losses, fewer violations.

## Standing measurement, stated carefully

n=10 frozen episodes; 1-0 is the minimum passing margin. The gate's
claim is not "large improvement" — it is that the loop now
demonstrably moves: teacher factory → preference distillation →
executed episodes → promotion. π₂'s teacher can now be collected by a
search seeded from π₁'s behavior, which is the generational shift the
loop needs. The remaining gap to the benchmark (dsm fill) is the
target for the next teacher wave: oversample dual-shelf-mixed states
where π₁'s preference disagrees with the executed search choice.
